What the Data Says

  • Priced right, homes go fast. Four of the six sales went under contract in 9 to 22 days (average 16) and closed at 96% to 98% of list, or above list in the case of 10117 Sweetleaf.
  • Overpricing costs real money. 9669 Leland, the best match, launched at $729,900, sat 91 days, and sold for $660,000. 9589 Pine Lily took 123 days and sold $40,000 under its list price.
  • Overpriced homes sit. Active listings average 87 days, and every one listed above market has needed at least one price cut. 10180 Cypress Vine is at 201 days.
  • The pending comp needed a correction. 9615 Sweetleaf only went under contract at day 45 after a $25,000 reduction and a $10,000 concession offer.
  • Supply is building. 7 contracts in this comp set from January to September (under 1 per month) against 5 active listings works out to about 6 months of inventory, a balanced market where buyers have choices.
  • Season matters. Listing in November means fewer buyers through the holidays. A sharp price at launch is how we capture the serious ones who are shopping now.

Strengths & Considerations

Strengths

  • Roof and HVAC are only 4 years old, a major buyer and insurance advantage
  • LVP in living areas, move-in ready look
  • Flexible layout: 4 bedrooms, office and an upstairs suite
  • Conservation backyard with mature oak and privacy
  • Flood zone X per county tax records, so no required flood insurance
  • About 290 sq ft larger than 9669 Leland, the closest recent sale

Considerations

  • No private pool, while the highest-priced listings have one
  • Carpet in bedrooms and den versus all-hard-surface comps
  • Prior 187-day expired listing appears in the property history
  • Competing actives nearby on Cypress Vine and Sweetleaf
Before we list: verify the heated square footage and correct the flood zone to X on the new MLS listing. The 2023 listing showed zone AE, and buyers who saw that history may assume flood insurance is required. Having the tax record ready to share will clear that up quickly.
Optional value boost: replacing the bedroom and den carpet with matching LVP would give buyers a fully hard-surface home and help the listing compete with renovated homes nearby. It could support pricing toward the upper end of the range.
